# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Hiring managers, tech leads and recruiters at technology companies in Spain who are evaluating Alex for a senior engineering or Tech Lead role. They arrive from LinkedIn or a direct link, skim in under a minute, and may print or download the CV.

## Product Purpose
Alex Nolasco's personal site: a portfolio (index.html) and a web CV (cv.html). Success is a hiring manager understanding in one screen what Alex leads, at what scale, and with which technologies, then contacting him.

## Positioning
A Tech Lead who owns a production data platform for telecom networks end to end (architecture, ETL, storage, deployment, observability, AI for operations), with measured results from Redmine and git, plus his own published open source.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static HTML/CSS/JS on GitHub Pages (kalexnolasco.github.io); no build step, no framework.
- Spanish first, English through the existing data-i18n switch.
- Light and dark themes through CSS tokens.
- The CV must print to a clean one-page A4 PDF.
- Public page: contact is email and LinkedIn only. No phone number on the web or in the public PDFs.
- No client names, salary or contract numbers anywhere.

## Brand Commitments
Name shown as Kevin Alexander Nolasco Pajuelo (Alex Nolasco). The incumbent portfolio's teal accent, Inter type and calm, technical voice. No photo on the CV.

## Evidence on Hand
- Figures measured 2026-11-20: 8+ years, 9,000+ commits in 55 repos, 12,000+ hours on 630+ tasks in 70 projects, 13 network vendors, ~17,000 tests, 99.99 % parity in the v4 migration, 7 MCP servers / 86 tools.
- Education: Bachelor's in Telematics Engineering and Bachelor's in Mathematics, two separate degrees (UIB, 2013–2020), never a "double degree"; exchange at Universidad de Guadalajara (2018, Santander scholarship); Astronomer Apache Airflow 3 Fundamentals (2025).
- Real technology logos (SVG) for the stack.

## Product Principles
- Short beats complete: one line per achievement, a number where there is one.
- Every claim is true and measurable.
- Technology shown as the real thing (logos and a diagram of the platform), always with its name as text.
